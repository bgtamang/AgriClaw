# Demo data

All files are synthetic, generated by `make_demo.R` (base R, seeds 7 and 8):

```
Rscript demo/make_demo.R
```

- `demo_ld_012.csv.gz`: 150 doubled-haploid lines × 900 SNPs (300 on each of 3 chromosomes of 10 Mb), coded 0/2, with 1% missing calls. The layout is the AgriClaw marker table (`marker, chr, pos`, then one column per line).
  - The lines were sampled from a forward simulation: 3 inbred founders, then 20 generations of random mating among 400 gametes, with Poisson crossovers (Haldane map).
  - The chromosomes have recombination rates of 0.5, 1 and 2 cM/Mb, so LD should decay over roughly 4, 2 and 1 times the same distance.
- `demo_truth.csv`: the recombination rate of each chromosome.

Positions and marker names are random and do not correspond to any real genome.
