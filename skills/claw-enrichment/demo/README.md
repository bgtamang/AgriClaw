# Demo data

Synthetic files generated by `make_demo.R` (fixed seed 2026). Re-create them with:

```
Rscript demo/make_demo.R
```

- `de_results.csv`: a DE table in the claw-de-analysis layout (`gene_id`, `baseMean`, `log2FoldChange`, `padj`) for 3000 invented genes (`Demo.01G000100` style). 2400 genes were "tested" (have a `padj`); 600 were "filtered" (`padj` empty). 150 genes are up- and 150 down-regulated (padj < 0.01).
- `go_annotation.tsv`: one row per gene with comma-separated GO IDs, holding only the most specific terms, like the `GO` column of a Phytozome `annotation_info.txt` file. About 28% of genes have no annotation. The GO IDs are real (checked against GO.db 3.23); the gene-term assignments are invented.
  - Planted for up-regulated genes (assigned to 30% of up genes vs 1.5% of others): GO:0009414 response to water deprivation, GO:0042631 cellular response to water deprivation, GO:0009737 response to abscisic acid, GO:0009269 response to desiccation.
  - Planted for down-regulated genes: GO:0009765 and GO:0009768 (photosynthesis, light harvesting), GO:0016168 chlorophyll binding, GO:0009535 chloroplast thylakoid membrane, GO:0009523 photosystem II.
  - 36 other BP/MF/CC terms are assigned at random (null terms).
  - GO:0018298 (obsolete in the GO release used by GO.db 3.23) appears on 5 genes, to show the obsolete-ID warning.
